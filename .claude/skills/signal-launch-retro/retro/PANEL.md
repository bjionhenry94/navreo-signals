# Mock-launch panel — 5 non-technical founders & sales leaders

Each persona walks the PROCESS.md mock launch end-to-end on paper (zero credits, nothing
sends) and scores /10 on Simplicity and Ease-of-launch, with a worst-moment quote.

Cast (fresh, non-technical, impatient): Fiona (creative-agency founder) · Marcus
(recruitment MD) · Renee (PR firm owner, terrified of accidental sends) · Tunde (VP Sales,
consultancy, protective of copy) · Priya (design-studio founder, first-time cold-emailer).

## Round 1

| Persona | Simplicity | Ease | Worst moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiona | 9 | 9 | "The card was great. The four silent minutes made me wonder if it died." |
| Marcus | 8 | 8 | "310 credits means nothing to me. Is that lunch money or a mortgage?" |
| Renee | 9 | 9 | "I needed the words 'nothing has been sent' one more time at the end. Say it twice." |
| Tunde | 8 | 7 | "I'm approving a price before I've read a single email you'll send in my name?" |
| Priya | 9 | 8 | "What's a 'copy source campaign'? Show me the email, don't name its ancestor." |
| **Avg** | **8.6** | **8.2** | — |

**Below bar on both axes. Three fixes applied to PROCESS.md:**
1. **The Card shows one rendered example email** (the copy source's best variant with the
   icebreaker filled for a real-looking company), tap to expand. Kills Tunde's and Priya's
   worst moments without adding a fourth decision — copy reading happens inside Decision 1.
2. **The Price line carries cash**: "310 credits (≈ £22) buys about 270 people you can email."
3. **The machinery ticks**: one plain line per stage ("Finding people… 287 found · Checking
   emails… 270 good"), and the Handover opens with "**Nothing has been sent.** It sends only
   when you press Start."
