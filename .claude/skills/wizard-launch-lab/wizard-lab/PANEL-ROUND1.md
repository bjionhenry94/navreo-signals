## Verdict

No prototype clears 9/10 on all three axes. Fix list for round 2:
1. **Shared** — "Who we email" edits must visibly change the number too (Fiona; the F5 rule applies to both groups).
2. **Shared** — caption wording simplified to "estimate · re-checked free before launch" (Tunde).
3. **L2 + L3** — preview gains ONE clamped offer line (Dana/Marcus/Fiona/Renee; biggest axis miss on the board).
4. **L1** — clamped facts get a visible expand cue (Marcus).
5. **L3** — disabled runway stops get a visibly dead state (Tunde); Go label carries "nothing sends" reassurance via the sign-off screen it opens (Renee — covered there, no extra copy on the card).
6. **L1 chip wall (Dana)** — noted, NOT fixed: hiding chips would hide the decision surface (over-minimisation is a defect too, minimalist-lab lesson). The wall IS the honest size of a 34-role signal.
