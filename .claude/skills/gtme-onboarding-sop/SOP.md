# Navreo Tool — GTME Field Guide

**Who this is for:** you've just joined as a Go-To-Market Engineer (GTME). This guide gets you delivering for clients **inside the Navreo tool** on day one.

**How to read it:** each task is one line of *what & when*, a short *do this*, and a 🎥 *video* that shows you the whole thing. Read the text to know **which page to open**; watch the video to know **exactly how**. You can skim the whole guide in one sitting.

---

## 0 · How the tool works (60 seconds)

You don't click your way through Navreo. You **talk to it in plain English in the chat**, and the **tool is your cockpit** — the place where everything lands and where you take action.

- **You type what you want** ("this campaign is running dry, top it up") → the work happens → **the result shows up on a page in the tool.**
- Five places on the left rail, that's the whole map:

| Rail button | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **Campaigns** | Every campaign, a health scoreboard, and your **daily to-do** (optimisations) |
| **Lists** | Every **prospect list** you've built + where you **add more prospects** |
| **Analytics** | **Account health** — deliverability, reply/bounce, mailboxes |
| **Setter** | Your **AI-SDRs** — they draft replies to people who respond |
| **Settings** | Your account bits |

> **⚖️ Chat or website — your choice.** Everything in this guide can be done two ways: by **talking to a chat**, or by **clicking around the website**. Use whichever is easier for you. (For campaign changes we recommend doing them **in-app**.)

## Setup — do this once (5 minutes)

Pin **three chats** you'll open every day. Add each one and pin it so it's always one click away.

| Pin this chat | Use it every day to… | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **/navreo-inbox** | Manage the inbox — read and handle replies | Your daily inbox home |
| **/navreo-analytics** | Check deliverability & understand the account at a **high level** | Account-wide view |
| **/navreo-campaigns** | Get **campaign-by-campaign** insights (deeper than account-level Analytics) | Make the actual changes **in-app** |

**How they differ:** `/navreo-analytics` is the **account-level** picture (is the whole account healthy?). `/navreo-campaigns` zooms into **each campaign** and tells you what to change — then you make that change in the app.

# PART 1 — YOUR DAILY 5

The five things you'll do most. Master these and you can run a client.

---

## 1 · Check for campaign optimisations (every morning)

**What & when:** first thing, every day — the tool tells you which campaigns need a tweak and what the tweak is. Your `/navreo-campaigns` chat gives the campaign-by-campaign read.

**Do this:**
1. Open **Campaigns** (or your **/navreo-campaigns** chat).
2. Work the **action cards / to-do** at the top — each is a suggested fix in plain English (swap a losing version, pause a dead angle, top up leads…).
3. Click a card → it opens the **Optimise cockpit** with the change teed up.
4. Approve the change, or ask in chat if you're unsure. Clear the list. **Make the changes in-app.**

**What good looks like:** the to-do is empty (or every item is deliberately left). You've actioned every same-day optimisation.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "The morning optimisation run" (4 min)**
> - Opening Campaigns / the /navreo-campaigns chat and reading the health scoreboard
> - What an action card is telling you and how to trust it
> - Clicking into the Optimise cockpit → approving a version swap in-app
> - When to just do it vs. when to ask in chat first
> - Finishing: clearing the to-do for every client you cover

## 2 · Daily account-health check

**What & when:** once a day — make sure the sending accounts are healthy so emails keep landing in inboxes. Your `/navreo-analytics` chat gives the high-level read.

**Do this:**
1. Open **Analytics** (or your **/navreo-analytics** chat).
2. Scan the top: **reply rate**, **bounce rate**, **in-warm-up / held** mailboxes.
3. Look at the **Inbox & Domain Manager** inline — anything flagged red needs action (the tool spells it out).
4. If something's off, work it right there in the Inbox & Domain Manager (deeper follow-ups → task 13).

**What good looks like:** bounce is low, reply rate is steady, no un-explained red mailboxes.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "The 5-minute health check" (4 min)**
> - The three numbers that matter: reply rate, bounce rate, held mailboxes
> - Reading the Inbox & Domain Manager — what red vs amber means
> - The client + date-range filters (7 / 14 / 30 days)
> - What to do when a domain gets flagged (and what to leave alone)

## 3 · Launch a new campaign

**What & when:** a client needs a new outreach campaign built and set live.

**Do this:**
1. In chat, say what you want: *"Launch a campaign for [client] targeting [who], offering [offer]."*
2. The tool opens the **Strategy walkthrough** — a step-by-step page that shows the reach, the audience, the first line, and the emails.
3. Walk the steps, approve the copy and targeting.
4. On launch it **opens the new campaign on its Overview page** so you can see it's ready.

**What good looks like:** the campaign appears in **Campaigns**, shows leads loaded and a green status.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Zero to live campaign" (6 min)**
> - The one sentence to type to start a launch
> - Walking the Strategy steps: reach → audience → first line → the emails
> - Approving copy vs. asking for changes in chat
> - Hitting live and landing on the campaign Overview
> - The proof it worked: leads loaded, status green

---

## 4 · Find prospects you've already added

**What & when:** before you build or add anyone — check who we've already got so you never double-contact.

**Do this:**
1. Open **Lists** to browse/search every prospect list already pulled (by client, niche, source).
2. To check a specific company or person, ask in chat: *"Have we ever contacted anyone at [company]?"* — you'll get a straight yes/no with the campaign.
3. To see who's inside a specific campaign, open that campaign → **Leads** tab.

**What good looks like:** you can answer "do we already have these people?" in under a minute, before spending on new data.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Who do we already have?" (3 min)**
> - Searching the Lists page by client / niche / source
> - The chat question that checks contact history instantly
> - Opening a campaign's Leads tab to see who's enrolled
> - Why you always check this first (no double-contacting)

---

## 5 · Add new prospects to an existing campaign

**What & when:** a campaign is running dry and needs fresh leads topped in.

**Do this:**
1. Open **Lists → Sources** for that campaign and use **Pull More** to get fresh, matching prospects, **or** say in chat *"top up [campaign] with fresh leads."*
2. Review the pulled prospects; the **upload gate opens in the chat** for you to confirm.
3. Confirm the upload → the new leads are added and a **record shows on the campaign's Leads page** (it tells you if only part of the pool went in).

**What good looks like:** the campaign's lead count goes up, and the Leads page shows the new upload record.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Topping up a dry campaign" (5 min)**
> - Spotting a campaign that's running out (where the tool flags it)
> - Sources → Pull More to get matching prospects
> - The upload gate in chat — reviewing and confirming
> - Checking the Leads page for the upload record
> - What "only part of the pool uploaded" means and what to do

## 6 · Get prospects — a niche list, or verified people at named companies  →  **/lilly-tam**

Two flavours of the same job — both live in **/lilly-tam**, and both land on the **Lists** page, company-matched, with verified emails.

**A · Build a niche list** — "build me a list of [freight forwarders / MSPs / roofers]." The most common request.
> In **/lilly-tam**, ask for the niche + geo → review the pulled list → it lands on **Lists**, ready.

**B · Pull & verify decision-makers at a list of companies** — "pull the DMs for these companies and verify the emails."
> In **/lilly-tam**, give it the companies → it finds the right decision-makers **and verifies their emails** → they land on **Lists**, ready to add to a campaign.

# PART 3 — THE SETTER (YOUR AI-SDRs)

The Setter is your team of **AI sales reps**. They watch for replies and draft the response so no conversation goes cold. Manage your daily inbox from **/navreo-inbox**; manage the agents themselves here. Four things to know, in order.

---

## S1 · Create an agent (do it in chat)

**What & when:** a client or a set of campaigns needs its own AI-SDR.

**Do this:** in chat — *"create a setter agent for [client] on [campaigns]."* It asks for the brief — the **pricing**, the **resources** to offer (Loom, Calendly, breakdown doc…), and **when to send each** — then builds the agent and assigns it to those campaigns.

**What good looks like:** the agent exists, is assigned to the right campaigns, and knows what to offer and when.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Create a setter agent" (4 min)**
> - The one ask to spin up an agent
> - The brief it needs: pricing, resources, when to send each
> - Assigning it to the right campaigns

---

## S2 · Train the AI-SDRs

**What & when:** make the agent sound like us before it goes near a real reply.

**Do this:** share the **training link** so example replies can be approved or redrafted, and teach **standing corrections** in chat (*"always offer the demo, never quote price on the first reply"*). It remembers what you teach it.

**What good looks like:** drafts come back in our tone, and a correction you taught once stops coming back.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Train your AI-SDRs" (4 min)**
> - Sharing the training link
> - Approving / redrafting example replies to teach tone
> - Teaching a standing correction that sticks
> - Checking the next draft reflects it

---

## S3 · Draft responses

**What & when:** day to day, as replies come in.

**Do this:** open **Setter**. For each incoming reply it shows a **drafted response** — read it, edit if needed, approve to send. Newest-relevant first.

**What good looks like:** every reply has a good draft; you're reviewing and approving, not writing from scratch.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Drafting & approving responses" (4 min)**
> - The Setter queue: what needs a reply first
> - Reading a drafted reply, editing, approving to send
> - When to escalate instead of send

---

## S4 · Add them to automated follow-ups

**What & when:** a lead goes quiet and needs chasing without you remembering to.

**Do this:** in chat or from the conversation — *"add this to automated follow-ups."* The agent keeps nudging on a schedule until they reply or the sequence ends.

**What good looks like:** quiet leads get chased automatically, and you can see a follow-up actually fired.

> 🎥 **VIDEO — "Automated follow-ups" (3 min)**
> - Turning on automated follow-ups for a conversation
> - The cadence and when it stops
> - Checking a follow-up actually fired
