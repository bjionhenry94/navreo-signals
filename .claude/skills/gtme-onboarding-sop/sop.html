Chat or website — your choice. Everything here can be done two ways: by talking to a chat, or by clicking around the website. Use whichever is easier for you. For campaign changes we recommend doing them in-app.

How the tool works — 60 seconds

You don't click your way through Navreo. You talk to it in plain English in the chat, and the tool is your cockpit — where everything lands and where you take action.

You type what you want → the work happens → the result shows up on a page. Five places on the left rail, that's the whole map:

CampaignsEvery campaign, a health scoreboard, and your daily to-do (optimisations)
ListsEvery prospect list you've built + where you add more prospects
AnalyticsAccount health — deliverability, reply / bounce, mailboxes
SetterYour AI-SDRs — they draft replies to people who respond
SettingsYour account bits

Part 1 — Your Daily 5

The five things you'll do most. Master these and you can run a client.

1

Check for campaign optimisations

Every morning — the tool tells you which campaigns need a tweak. Your /navreo-campaigns chat gives the campaign-by-campaign read.

  1. Open Campaigns (or your /navreo-campaigns chat).
  2. Work the action cards / to-do at the top — each is a suggested fix in plain English (swap a losing version, pause a dead angle, top up leads…).
  3. Click a card → it opens the Optimise cockpit with the change teed up.
  4. Approve it, or ask in chat if unsure. Clear the list. Make the changes in-app.
What good looks like: the to-do is empty (or every item deliberately left). Every same-day optimisation actioned.

  • What an action card is telling you and how to trust it
  • Clicking into the Optimise cockpit → approving a version swap in-app
  • When to just do it vs. when to ask in chat first
  • Finishing: clearing the to-do for every client you cover
2

Daily account-health check

Once a day — keep sending accounts healthy so emails land. Your /navreo-analytics chat gives the high-level read.

  1. Open Analytics (or your /navreo-analytics chat).
  2. Scan the top: reply rate, bounce rate, in-warm-up / held mailboxes.
  3. Look at the Inbox & Domain Manager inline — anything red needs action (the tool spells it out).
  4. If something's off, work it right there in the Inbox & Domain Manager (deeper follow-ups → task 13).
What good looks like: bounce is low, reply rate steady, no un-explained red mailboxes.
▶Video · The 5-minute health check4 min

  • Reading the Inbox & Domain Manager — what red vs amber means
  • The client + date-range filters (7 / 14 / 30 days)
  • What to do when a domain gets flagged (and what to leave alone)
3

Launch a new campaign

When a client needs a new outreach campaign built and set live.

  1. In chat, say what you want: "Launch a campaign for [client] targeting [who], offering [offer]."
  2. The tool opens the Strategy walkthrough — reach, audience, first line, the emails.
  3. Walk the steps, approve the copy and targeting.
  4. On launch it opens the new campaign on its Overview page.
What good looks like: the campaign appears in Campaigns, leads loaded, status green.
▶Video · Zero to live campaign6 min
  • The one sentence to type to start a launch
  • Walking the Strategy steps: reach → audience → first line → the emails
  • Approving copy vs. asking for changes in chat
  • Hitting live and landing on the campaign Overview
  • The proof it worked: leads loaded, status green
4

Find prospects you've already added

Before you build or add anyone — check who we've already got so you never double-contact.

  1. Open Lists to browse/search every prospect list already pulled (by client, niche, source).
  2. To check a specific company/person, ask in chat: "Have we ever contacted anyone at [company]?" — straight yes/no with the campaign.
  3. To see who's inside a campaign, open it → Leads tab.
What good looks like: you can answer "do we already have these people?" in under a minute, before spending on new data.
▶Video · Who do we already have?3 min
  • Searching the Lists page by client / niche / source
  • The chat question that checks contact history instantly
  • Opening a campaign's Leads tab to see who's enrolled
  • Why you always check this first (no double-contacting)
5

Add new prospects to an existing campaign

When a campaign is running dry and needs fresh leads topped in.

  1. Open Lists → Sources and use Pull More, or say in chat "top up [campaign] with fresh leads."
  2. Review the pulled prospects; the upload gate opens in the chat to confirm.
  3. Confirm → new leads added, and a record shows on the campaign's Leads page (it tells you if only part of the pool went in).
What good looks like: the campaign's lead count goes up, and the Leads page shows the new upload record.

Part 2 — The wider toolkit

Things you'll do several times a week. Same rule: ask in chat, check the page. Each has a video.

6

Get prospects — a niche list, or verified people at named companies

Two flavours of the same job — both live in /lilly-tam, and both land on the Lists page, company-matched, with verified emails.

A · Build a niche list — in /lilly-tam: "build me a list of freight forwarders / MSPs / roofers." Ask for the niche + geo → review → lands on Lists.
B · Verify DMs at a company list — in /lilly-tam: "pull the DMs for these companies and verify the emails." It finds the right decision-makers and verifies their emails → lands on Lists.

Part 3 — The Setter (your AI-SDRs)

Your team of AI sales reps. They watch for replies and draft the response so no conversation goes cold.

Manage your daily inbox from /navreo-inbox; manage the agents themselves here. Four things to know, in order.
S1

Create an agent (do it in chat)

When a client or set of campaigns needs its own AI-SDR.

  1. In chat — "create a setter agent for [client] on [campaigns]."
  2. It asks for the brief — the pricing, the resources to offer (Loom, Calendly, breakdown doc…), and when to send each.
  3. It builds the agent and assigns it to those campaigns.
What good looks like: the agent exists, is assigned to the right campaigns, and knows what to offer and when.
▶Video · Create a setter agent4 min
  • The one ask to spin up an agent
  • The brief it needs: pricing, resources, when to send each
  • Assigning it to the right campaigns
S2

Train the AI-SDRs

Before it goes near a real reply — make the agent sound like us.

  1. Share the training link so example replies can be approved or redrafted.
  2. Teach standing corrections in chat ("always offer the demo, never quote price on the first reply").
  3. It remembers what you teach it.
What good looks like: drafts come back in our tone, and a correction you taught once stops coming back.
▶Video · Train your AI-SDRs4 min
  • Sharing the training link
  • Approving / redrafting example replies to teach tone
  • Teaching a standing correction that sticks
  • Checking the next draft reflects it
S3

Draft responses

Day to day, as replies come in.

  1. Open Setter. For each incoming reply it shows a drafted response.
  2. Read it, edit if needed, approve to send. Newest-relevant first.
What good looks like: every reply has a good draft; you're reviewing and approving, not writing from scratch.
▶Video · Drafting & approving responses4 min
  • The Setter queue: what needs a reply first
  • Reading a drafted reply, editing, approving to send
  • When to escalate instead of send
S4

Add them to automated follow-ups

When a lead goes quiet and needs chasing without you remembering to.

  1. In chat or from the conversation — "add this to automated follow-ups."
  2. The agent keeps nudging on a schedule until they reply or the sequence ends.
What good looks like: quiet leads get chased automatically, and you can see a follow-up actually fired.
▶Video · Automated follow-ups3 min
  • Turning on automated follow-ups for a conversation
  • The cadence and when it stops
  • Checking a follow-up actually fired
