# Research Brief: Gojiberry ($3.5M ARR in ~11 months) and its founders' GTM playbook

Run date: 2026-07-20. Sources: gojiberry.ai live pages (via render proxy; direct fetch 403s), YC company page, founder X posts (search-indexed; x.com blocked direct reads), Product Hunt, Maven, blog.gojiberry.ai, and independent reviews/case studies. Every finding carries its URL. Full agent reports live in the session transcript.

## (a) Pain points they lead with

1. Reply rates stuck at 2-3% with "no real system behind it": traditional outbound has "poor targeting and lack of systematic learning across campaigns". https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gojiberry-ai
2. The SDR cost stack: "$70k before commissions, benefits, training, equipment, and turnover", capped at 8 hours a day, ending in "a CRM packed with leads that have long gone cold while the team burns out on manual prospecting". https://blog.gojiberry.ai/blog/ai-sdr
3. Fragmented guesswork stack: "buy tools, hire SDRs and agencies, test lists, write messages, and guess what works". https://www.productmarketfit.tech/p/gojiberry-yc-p26-pitch-deck-how-scaled
4. The ask itself is the flaw in most cold email: "You are asking for something from them. And that's the main problem." (Roman, founders' cold-email teardown). https://alexberman.com/cold-email-blueprint-strategy-reply-rates
5. "4 in 5 B2B buyers ignore unsolicited LinkedIn messages in 2026." https://maven.com/p/7a6cb7/3x-your-linked-in-reply-rate-ft-gojiberry-ai
6. "Most deals die in the follow-up": the email that never gets sent. https://blog.gojiberry.ai/blog/follow-up-email-generator

## (b) Value props

7. Hero line (near-verbatim our magnet promise): "Your AI agent finds high intent leads and contacts them for you... Your sales agent runs 24/7. And gets better every week." https://gojiberry.ai
8. "Start targeting buyers already in buying mode": signal-first, not database-first. https://www.producthunt.com/products/gojiberry
9. "One agent. Replaces your entire outreach stack." No sequences to build. https://gojiberry.ai
10. Capacity reallocation, not headcount threat: "Founders can create pipeline without hiring SDRs; existing teams can redirect reps from prospecting to selling and closing." https://gojiberry.ai/faq
11. "Outbound that feels like inbound." https://superframeworks.com/case-study/gojiberry

## (c) Offer mechanics

12. Pricing: free trial (no credit card), Pro $99/mo ("2 AI agents prospecting 24/7"), custom tier above. Undercuts Artisan ($2.4-7.2k/mo) and 11x ($5-10k/mo) by 25-100x. https://gojiberry.ai
13. Time-to-value as implicit risk reversal: "3 minutes to set up. First results today." / "Live in 5 minutes." https://gojiberry.ai
14. NO real risk reversal exists: terms say "All subscription fees are non-refundable, except as required by law." No money-back guarantee, no outcome pricing, no work-free-until clause anywhere in the category leader's offer. https://gojiberry.ai/general-terms
15. Cost contrast lives in FAQ/reviews, not the hero: $99/mo vs $65K-120K/yr tools vs a $70k+ SDR. https://prospeo.io/s/gojiberry-ai-pricing-reviews-pros-and-cons
16. Testimonials do the guarantee's job: "We made our money back 6x already" (Stuart Brent, SaasyDB). https://gojiberry.ai

## (d) Proof, ICP, and the founders' magnet playbook

17. Proof pattern = one name + one number: "15+ demos per week" (Wipra), "+31% reply rate" (Mindflow), "100+ meetings" (GTE Localize), "5 demos from 30 leads" (KubaLabs). Never blended stats. https://gojiberry.ai/case-study
18. Growth: $0 to $3.5M ARR in 11 months (founder post), 2,000+ customers, profitable, 30% MoM. https://x.com/pierreeliottlal/status/2071870616135676153 and https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gojiberry-ai
19. Magnet formats: "Comeback Kit" follow-up templates (Product Hunt), free Follow-Up Email Generator, comment-gated "cloud-powered prospecting system" blueprint delivered by DM, free Maven lightning lesson "3x Your LinkedIn Reply Rate". https://www.producthunt.com/products/the-follow-up-kit-that-closes-deals
20. Dylan gave away "42 GTM agents built on Claude Cowork... And you can have all of them. Free." Direct market validation that GTM-inside-Claude is a magnet buyers want. https://www.linkedin.com/in/dylan-txa/
21. The reply mechanic: give the full mechanism first, make the ask "three letters max to reply" ("just reply yes"), defer the meeting entirely. Credited with 10x-ing reply rates. https://alexberman.com/cold-email-blueprint-strategy-reply-rates
22. ICP where they win: founders and small B2B sales teams, agencies, SaaS, consulting, professional services; LinkedIn-active verticals. https://runwhatworks.com/blog/gojiberry-ai-review

## What this means for our angles

1. **Our magnet framing is pre-validated.** The fixed CTA promise (high-intent leads from inside Claude/ChatGPT, runs 24/7, gets better every week) is nearly Gojiberry's hero line, proven at $3.5M ARR. Do not soften it.
2. **Genuine risk reversal is an open gap in the category.** The leader is contractually non-refundable; their "risk reversal" is a free trial plus testimonials. Our pay-after-build angle owns something nobody in-category offers. (Seeded Angle 1 confirmed.)
3. **An outcome guarantee is fully differentiated.** Nobody in the category guarantees a number. "30 qualified leads in 90 days or you don't pay" must define "qualified" in-line to stay credible. (Seeded Angle 2 confirmed.)
4. **Claude-native is a live wave, not a curiosity.** A Gojiberry founder used free Claude GTM agents as a magnet. "Runs inside the Claude/ChatGPT your team already uses, no new tool, no new hire" is the evidence-picked Angle 3, with the SDR cost contrast as its supporting clause (capacity framing first, blunt cost math second, per their FAQ pattern).
5. **Speed with a number is the category's trust currency.** "3 minutes to set up, first results today" does guarantee-work without a guarantee. "Live inside a week, first qualified conversations in two weeks" is the evidence-picked Angle 4.
6. **The email mechanics:** offer paragraph carries the pitch, magnet CTA gives the full method away unconditionally, ask stays one small yes. Proof lines follow one-name-one-number, never blended stats.
