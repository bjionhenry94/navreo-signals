## Why the stop is honest, not premature

1. Every copy-side fix the panel requested was applied and moved scores (A2 +2.0, A1 +3.0 over the lab). What remains on A1/A2 is not wording: it is price, make-good mechanics, go-live date, and same-stage proof. Those are commercial decisions only Bjion can make.

3. A3 and A4 were rewritten three times, absorbed both bench candidates, and never earned a single would-reply from 20 buyers. The evidence says they are supporting material (anchors, mechanism lines), not standalone cold offers.
