# Angles Draft: four offers, one variable

Run date: 2026-07-20. Engine rules applied: ONE mechanism per offer, 40-word offer statement, email-ready paragraph, soft yes-CTA, no audit offers, no em-dashes, avoid "your size".

## The shared skeleton (identical across all four variants)

Subject: `Quick one {{first_name}}`

```
Hi {{first_name}},

{{Icebreaker}}

[OFFER PARAGRAPH: the only thing that changes]

Either way, I put the whole method into a free training: how to find
high-intent leads and contacts from inside Claude or ChatGPT, with a
system that runs 24/7 and gets better every week. Can I send it over?

Best,
%signature%

P.S. We've done this for 50+ clients and helped one of Europe's
fastest-growing agencies scale to 50 calls a day.
```

The magnet CTA is unconditional ("Either way"): the guide is the whole first ask, the offer paragraph just plants the flag. Ask stays one small yes, per the founders' "three letters max" mechanic. Per-angle proof lines below are for Email 2 of the sequence, NOT the A/B email; the P.S. stays fixed so only the offer paragraph varies.

## Angle 1: Build-First (risk reversal, seeded + proven)

**Positioning line:** Nobody in this category refunds anything. We charge nothing until the engine exists.

**40-word offer statement:** We build {{company_name}} a complete AI outbound engine that spots buyers already showing intent and reaches out for you. Nothing upfront and nothing while we build. Your first payment only happens after it is built and running.

**Mechanism (one):** we build first, you pay after.

**Offer paragraph:**
> If we built {{company_name}} an AI outbound engine that finds buyers already showing intent and reaches out for you, adding qualified leads every month without you hiring anyone, would you be interested? You only pay after we've built it, so nothing upfront.

**Proof line (Email 2 use):** The same offer paragraph carried 188k sends for us and our clients; one agency client scaled to 50 calls a day.

## Angle 2: 30-in-90 Guarantee (outcome, seeded)

**Positioning line:** The category sells trials and testimonials. We put a number on it and eat the risk.

**40-word offer statement:** We run {{company_name}}'s outbound end to end and guarantee 30 qualified leads inside 90 days. Miss the number and you do not pay. A qualified lead means a decision maker at a fit company replying with real interest.

**Mechanism (one):** a guaranteed number with a no-pay miss clause.

**Offer paragraph:**
> We'd run {{company_name}}'s outbound end to end and guarantee 30 qualified leads in your first 90 days. If we miss the number, you don't pay. Every lead is a decision maker who replied with interest, not a name on a list.

**Proof line (Email 2 use):** Clients have booked calls with buyers at Samsung, BCG and HubSpot through this exact engine.
