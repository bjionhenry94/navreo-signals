## Problem statements

**P1 (hiring):**
> Adding pipeline usually means adding headcount: a loaded SDR runs about $75k a year before they book a single call.

**P2 (intent):**
> Most cold outreach gets mediocre replies because everyone is blasting the same lists. We only contact decision makers already showing buying signals (hiring, funding, LinkedIn activity), so replies come back warmer.

## Offers

**O1 (build now, pay later):**
> We'd build {{company_name}} a complete outbound engine across email and LinkedIn, and carry the whole build ourselves: every message drafted for your sign-off. You pay nothing upfront and nothing while we build. Billing starts only after your tenth qualified reply, and if they never land, you never pay.

**O2 (30-in-90):**
> We'd run {{company_name}}'s outbound across email and LinkedIn on a simple deal: 30 qualified leads in your first 90 days or you don't pay. Qualified means a decision maker from a list you approved, replying to ask about working together. You sign off every message, and every reply lands in your own inbox, so you're the one keeping count.
