# Own Proof: what Navreo already knows converts

Run date: 2026-07-20. Source: Supabase reply archive + live Smartlead analytics (pulled 2026-07-20), all-time retro of the "pay after we've built it" template family.

## The proven winning line (verbatim, from campaign_versions)

> "If we could build you an AI lead-generation engine that added 30+ qualified leads every month, without having to hire a BDR team, would you be interested?
> **You only pay after we've built it, so {no|zero|absolutely no} upfront amount.**
> Can I send a one-pager showing how it works?"

Two-email sequence. Email 2 explains the mechanism (watches buying signals daily: recent hires, site visitors, LinkedIn engagement; AI qualifies, enriches, reaches out) plus one proof story, then a soft date ask.

## Lessons that carry into the new angles

1. Risk reversal WORKS as an offer paragraph. It survived 188k sends as the constant while audiences varied.
2. Audience quality moved results 6x more than anything else. Same words: 1-in-182 warm vs 1-in-1,125 cold. Offer testing must hold audience constant per niche.
3. The soft ask ("Can I send a one-pager?") outperformed meeting asks in the house's experience; every new angle keeps a soft magnet CTA.
4. A concrete number in the promise (30+ qualified leads / 50 calls a day / 50 clients) anchors the pitch.

## Claimable platform capabilities (for proof + mechanism lines)

From the Navreo signals platform (navreo-signals.onrender.com, the campaigns app):

1. Watches buying signals daily: hiring, funding, LinkedIn engagement, follower activity, site-relevant triggers.
2. Runs 24/7: auto-pull pipelines feed new leads and signals into campaigns continuously without a human touching it.
3. Gets better every week: a live learning loop feeds reply outcomes back into targeting and copy.
4. AI appointment setter drafts and manages replies inside the same system.
5. Suppression and exclusion checks are built in: nobody already contacted or blocked gets emailed again.
6. The whole thing is operated from inside Claude Code / ChatGPT, the tools the buyer's team already uses.
