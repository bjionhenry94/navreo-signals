## The lab's central finding (3 rounds, 15 judges, 60 verdicts)

Cold buyers reply to DEFINED-OUTCOME RISK TRANSFER. Every 8+ verdict in the lab (7 of 7) landed on the guarantee. Every "to make it 10" on the other angles asks them to borrow the guarantee's teeth: a number, a date, a definition, a consequence. Positioning stories without teeth cap at 4-6 ("just a description of the service"). Clean money mechanics without an anchor cap at 7 ("clean but unanchored").

**A2's remaining gap is not copy.** Four of five round-3 judges said price: "put a monthly number next to the guarantee and I'd reply right now" / "framed against what a fourth BDM costs so I can do the maths in the same email". Naming a price is a Bjion decision, not a lab decision. v4 adds the strongest honest substitute (price fixed in writing before anything sends); if A2 still lands ~7.5, the report ships it as the lead arm pending a price line.

Second recurring Bjion-side gap: same-stage named client proof (the Samsung/BCG/HubSpot buyer names impress but read as differently-sized; judges want "a firm like ours").

## v4 texts (round 4, final rewrite)

**A1 Build-First v4** (anchor the trigger with a count, contract grammar):
> We'd build {{company_name}} a complete outbound engine and carry the whole build ourselves: buying signals watched daily, decision makers found, every email drafted for your sign-off. You pay nothing upfront and nothing while we build. Billing starts only after your tenth qualified reply, a decision maker from your approved list replying to talk. If they never land, you never pay.

**A2 Guarantee v4** (price-transparency clause, the honest ceiling without a set price):
> We'd run {{company_name}}'s outbound on a simple deal: 30 qualified leads in your first 90 days or you don't pay. Qualified means a decision maker from a target list you approved upfront, replying to ask about working together. You sign off every email before it sends, every reply lands in your own inbox, and the monthly price is fixed in writing before anything goes out.

**A3 Cost-Collapse v4** (real anchor number on THEIR side, jargon cut, delivery condition):
> A loaded SDR runs about $75k a year before they book a single call. We'd run {{company_name}}'s whole outbound for a fraction of that: lists built, every email drafted for your sign-off, sending and follow-ups handled, deliverability protected. No new platform for your team to learn, no hire, no agency retainer, and you only pay while it's delivering.

**A4 Warm-Replies v4** (hook pivots from speed to reply quality + compounding, per three rounds of evidence; sign-off restored; micro-floor kept):
> Most cold outbound gets mediocre replies because everyone is blasting the same lists. Ours watches live buying signals (hiring, funding, LinkedIn activity) and only contacts decision makers already in motion, so replies come back warmer. It learns from every reply, so week eight beats week one. You approve every email before it sends, and if replies aren't coming, you're not paying.

Shared skeleton unchanged from v3 (welded CTA + named-buyers P.S.).
