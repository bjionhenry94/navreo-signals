Simulated panel of 5 non-technical founders & sales leaders. Each scored all 5 prototypes 1–10 on
**Actionable · Digest · Beauty**. Averages across the panel:

| Prototype (ideas HOME framework)        | Actionable | Digest | Beauty | **Avg** |
|-----------------------------------------|:---------:|:------:|:------:|:-------:|

| **P2 Board** (reach leaderboard)        |    7.0    |**8.2** |  7.0   |**7.40** |
| P1 Deck (card gallery)                  |    6.6    |  7.0   |  7.8   |  7.13   |
| P4 Canvas (swappable blocks)            |    6.6    |  6.0   |  6.4   |  6.33   |

Bar = 9/10 from **every** reviewer on **all three**. Round 1: **none clear the bar** (honest gap — same
pattern as launch-dashboard-uxlab's 8.5-vs-9.0). Two-horse race: **P5** (unanimous 9 on beauty; "a moment,
not a menu"; clearest single next step) and **P2** (most skimmable + decisive; trust tag on the face).

## Favourites
- non-technical founder → **P5** · VP Sales → **P2** · agency operator → **P2** (P5 close 2nd) · skeptical COO → **P2** · brand-led founder → **P5**

## The fixes every reviewer converged on (the recipe for a round-2 winner)
1. **Merge P5 + P2** — keep P5's cinematic single-idea hero, add P2's compact ranked compare so you can weigh all five *without leaving* the spotlight (4/5 asked P5 for exactly this).
2. **Add the "why it's worth it" one-liner** back onto the hero (P5 drops it; founder + brand want it).
3. **Rank by "worth it", not raw reach** — the 120 is *estimated* and unfairly tops a reach-only sort (VP Sales, COO, founder).
4. **Trust tag on the face** — explicit "real, checked" vs "estimated", not a subtle "~" (COO).
5. **Colour discipline** — collapse the green/blue/amber category pills to ONE neutral-ink treatment so orange is the only accent; drop the backdrop-blur header (agency operator — matches the Navreo "one accent, no glass" rule).
6. **Kill the kanban for idea *review*** — P3's build-stage lanes read as process theatre. Progress belongs as a *quiet* element (P5's sidebar mini-track), not the HOME.

P3 is the outlier the brief flagged wanting ("visualise progress") — but reviewers say a full kanban is the wrong home for it; keep progress subtle.
