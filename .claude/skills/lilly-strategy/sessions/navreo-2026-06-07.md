**Date:** 2026-06-07

## Recommended top 3 (where to start)

Ranked by (score × delivery-friction):

### 1. CRM consultancies who fix others' sales tech but not their own pipeline
- **What it is:** Salesforce / HubSpot implementation partners, 51-200 staff, across the 14-country set. 344 companies, cleanest niche in the probe set.
- **Why it'll work:** Perfect irony hook — they install the revenue stack for clients but their own new business is founder-led. Tight, named, easy to qualify. New Breed (HubSpot partner) is a textbook fit in the sample.
- **Lead magnet:** M1 Outbound teardown — "we mapped the 3 campaigns we'd run for [agency] + a sample cold email." DFY framing, soft yes-CTA.
- **Build chain:** `lilly-tam-mapper` (expand 344 → full list across Ocean+Prospeo) → `lilly-decision-maker-finder` (Prospeo enrich VP-Sales + Founder) → `lilly-bot`.
- **Approx. credit budget to launch:** ~10-30 Ocean/Prospeo (expansion) + ~1 Prospeo/company enriched (pilot 200 cos ≈ 200-400 cr).

### 2. Dev/AI agencies posting their FIRST sales / BD hire
- **What it is:** Software-dev / IT-consulting / AI firms in the geo set with an open posting for BDR / SDR / Head of Sales / Founding Sales / Head of Growth.
- **Why it'll work:** Hottest intent on the menu — the job post IS the buying trigger. They're at the exact moment of moving off founder-led sales; Navreo's pitch is "rent the system, get there in weeks not quarters."
- **Lead magnet:** M4 Founder-led-sales → system playbook (soft, value-first) for the opener; pair M1 teardown on follow-up.
- **Build chain:** `lilly-theirstack-setup` (TAM-test 4-6 title patterns, free) → `lilly-theirstack-data-processing`. Pass `skip_angles=["Hiring","You joined"]` to `lilly-icebreaker` at the data step (hiring-signal rule).
- **Approx. credit budget to launch:** TheirStack previews free; DM enrichment ~1 Prospeo/company on the daily flow.

### 3. AI-implementation firms scaling delivery, neglecting demand
- **What it is:** AI consulting / implementation firms (LeewayHertz-type), 51-200, geo set. 552 companies.
- **Why it'll work:** Hot category with budget; growing delivery teams fast while their own pipeline lags. Strong novelty.
- **Lead magnet:** M3 90-day GTM system plan (soft) or M1 teardown (DFY).
- **Build chain:** `lilly-tam-mapper` → `lilly-decision-maker-finder` → `lilly-bot`. Tighten keywords + WebFetch-verify on page 1 (moderate sample-fit, expect ~30-40% off-brief noise to drop).
- **Approx. credit budget to launch:** similar to Idea 1.

## Brainstormed but cut

- **Digital-transformation consultancies (broad)** — 3,996 cos but score 11/20: umbrella term, noisy sample (product cos like UpCloud leaked in), generic angle. Re-add only as a keyword-tightened sub-cut.
- **News / funding intent (recently-funded consultancies)** — ~12/20: most services firms are bootstrapped and don't raise, so the funding signal is thin for this ICP. Better for product/SaaS targets, which Navreo excludes.
- **Lookalikes of named clients** — named clients (Amplifyy/Amazon, Arnic/sales-enablement, Pathos/PR, TalentStream/staffing) don't sit in the 3 niche verticals, so a lookalike seed cluster would pull off-niche. Revisit if Navreo lands a dev/AI/transformation client to seed from.
