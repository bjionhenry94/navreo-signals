## Recommended top 3 (where to start)
Ranked by score × low delivery-friction.

### 1. Brands whose DTC traffic is falling — channel-diversify pitch (#1)
- **What it is:** Layer a traffic-DECLINE filter (down 15%+ quarter-on-quarter) onto the e-commerce brand base you already run. 9,777 brands qualify right now. This is the mirror image of the live surge campaign: instead of "you're growing, scale it on Amazon," the message is "your direct-to-consumer traffic is softening this quarter — Amazon is a channel that's still growing, and we'll launch/scale you there for you."
- **Why it'll work:** Biggest net-new pool on the menu (~29,300 target contacts) and a genuine reason-to-act. Pure filter swap on infrastructure that's already live — lowest build cost of anything here. Copy must stay constructive (diversification opportunity), never insulting about their numbers.
- **Lead magnet:** Amazon revenue-opportunity forecast (what their category earns on Amazon vs their current DTC-only run-rate).
- **Build chain:** Prospeo base + `website_traffic` decline filter → `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` → `/lilly-email-verification` → `/lilly-bot`.
- **Credit budget:** ~1 Prospeo credit/page on the pull; enrichment as normal. No new infrastructure.

### 2. Brands that just appointed a new senior leader (#2)
- **What it is:** Layer `company_key_execs` (Any C-Level Appointed, last 180 days) onto the same live base. 629 brands (732 including VPs) have new leadership right now. New leaders arrive with a reorg, fresh budget, and a mandate to prove wins fast.
- **Why it'll work:** Highest-intent Prospeo signal available, and buildable today (this filter was flagged dead earlier this morning but is live again — see the memory correction). Zero new infrastructure, one extra filter on a pull you already run. Open on the appointment ("congrats on the new CMO").
- **Lead magnet:** Category Amazon benchmark report — a value-first artefact a new leader can take to their team.
- **Build chain:** Prospeo base + `company_key_execs` → `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` → `/lilly-icebreaker` (`skip_angles=["You joined"]`).
- **Credit budget:** ~1 Prospeo credit/page. Negligible incremental.

### 3. Brands hiring an Amazon PPC / Advertising specialist (#3)
- **What it is:** Catch brands the moment they post a job for a dedicated Amazon-ads person. 34 companies/month, ~410/year. A brand staffing Amazon ads specifically is the single hottest intent signal for Kevin's exact service (managed Amazon advertising).
- **Why it'll work:** Perfect offer-to-signal fit — they are literally trying to hire the thing Amplifyy does for them on a performance basis. Small monthly flow, so run it as an always-on drip, not a volume play. The job post is the icebreaker ("saw you're hiring for Amazon ads — we run it for you so you don't have to").
- **Lead magnet:** Free done-for-you Amazon ad-account teardown (gift-card stays out of this email, per the copy constraint).
- **Build chain:** `/lilly-theirstack-setup` (pipeline already exists for the live hiring campaigns) → daily enrichment → `/lilly-icebreaker` (`skip_angles=["Hiring","You joined"]` — the signal IS hiring, so don't open on it).
- **Credit budget:** ~0 to add the saved search (TheirStack previews free); ~1 Prospeo credit/company/day at enrichment.
