## Method (no speculation)
- **Company base re-validated against yesterday:** `company_type{is_retail:true, subtypes:[E-commerce]}` + US/UK/CA/AU + headcount 1-500. Re-ran both company probes: #6 = 17,885, #7 = 4,451 — exact match to 2026-05-26, so DM counts sit on precisely the build segment.
- **DM counts = live `/search-person` `total_count`** on that exact segment + headcount band + person filter. Signal confirmed honored on `/search-person` (#6 Band-1 founders = 4,360 with surge filter vs 11,455 without).
- **4 bands per the title map**, founders vs operators kept as disjoint seniority sets (no double-count):
  - Founders/owners = `person_seniority:[Founder/Owner, C-Suite]`
  - Operators = `person_seniority:[Vice President, Head, Director]` + `person_department:[Marketing]`
- **Why Marketing-only for operators:** the first pass used Marketing+Sales+Ops and the title audit was full of non-Amazon roles (VP Field Sales, Store Director, Director of Logistics, Head of Purchasing, a misclassified Exec Assistant). Marketing-only isolates the real channel buyer (Digital/Brand/Growth/eCommerce-marketing leaders). Reported as the headline operator number; the broad Mktg+Sales+Ops figure is kept as an inflated upper bound.
- **Exact-title match is unusable here:** "head of amazon" fragments into 12+ canonical variants ("head of amazon operations", "global head of amazon"...), so seniority+department is the only reliable counting method.

## Caveats (honest bounds)
1. **Operator count is a Marketing-function proxy.** A small number of pure "Head of Amazon / Director of Marketplaces" roles may sit under Sales/Ops in Prospeo's taxonomy and are NOT in the headline operator number; conversely the broad Mktg+Sales+Ops figure over-counts with non-Amazon sales/ops roles. The truth sits between the headline and the ceiling, closer to the headline.
2. **Band-3 "owner sign-off" uses full C-suite** (catches CEO/President/COO/CMO but also CFO/CTO/CHRO). It's a secondary/sign-off layer, not the primary buyer at that size.
3. **`total_count` = findable in Prospeo's index.** Verified-email yield at the `lilly-decision-maker-finder` + `lilly-email-verification` step will trim this further (typically 60-85% email hit on Prospeo-source).

Confirm the persona stance so the copy and enrichment titles match.
