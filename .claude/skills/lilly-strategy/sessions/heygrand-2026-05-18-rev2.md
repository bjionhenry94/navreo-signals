## Phase 5, Recommended top 3 (where to start)

Ranked by (Total score × Ease):

### 1. Insolvency-list bait, phased rollout by sub-vertical (score 25 × ease 5 = 125)

- **What it is.** Send a 4-line email to Credit Controllers and Heads of Finance at T2/T3 UK construction firms in one sub-vertical at a time. The payload: a free list of construction companies in their geographic region that went into administration or insolvency this week, pulled directly from Grand's database. Roll out one sub-vertical at a time so we can A/B copy without contaminating the list: builders merchants first (highest density, clearest fit), then plant hire, then electrical wholesalers, then manufacturers.
- **Why it'll work.** Highest-fit signal across all 10 ideas. Credit controllers' #1 KPI is "don't extend credit to a firm that's about to go bust." The lead magnet IS their job. Refreshes weekly so the same campaign re-touches every week with fresh data: no exhaustion problem. Maps directly to Kirk's primary messaging anchor (stop-loss / risk-removal, not money savings).
- **Lead magnet.** M1 weekly regional insolvency list, delivered as a CSV per recipient geo.
- **Build chain on sign-off.** Filter 88K → `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` (Persona 1 + 2 titles, drop "Account Manager") → `/lilly-email-verification` (AI Ark primary, Prospeo fallback) → push to Instantly. No-email-found rows go to HeyReach via `/lilly-heyreach-upload`. Kirk's eng team produces a per-region insolvency CSV ahead of each weekly send.
- **Credit budget to launch.** ~200 Prospeo (DM enrichment of ~5K builders merchants T2/T3) + ~50 AI Ark (verification). Lead-magnet content costs zero (Grand's own data).

### 2. Trade-credit bait + personalised video (score 22 × ease 5 = 110)

- **What it is.** Scale Kirk's currently-winning email ("Hey, do you offer trade credit?") from ~2K leads to the full Cat 1 universe (~85K after MCR subtraction). On positive reply, instead of pitching the platform (which kills the conversation when they realise we're not a buyer), trigger a per-recipient personalised video that opens with "Hi {firstname}, I want to tell you how OrderD have been using Grand…"
- **Why it'll work.** Currently winning at small scale; just needs volume + the video-pivot to fix the bait-and-switch conversion gap. Kirk has been losing replies the moment recipients realise Grand isn't a credit applicant; the video reframes the conversation as a peer testimonial.
- **Lead magnet.** M6 video + M4 £500 free credits offer post-reply.
- **Build chain on sign-off.** Same TAM chain as #1, full Cat 1 minus the 5K used in #1. Kirk records one ~3min base video; [Pitchlane](https://pitchlane.com/) or [escript](https://escript.com/) does per-recipient name-swap. Manual trigger on positive reply for first 2 weeks, then automate.
- **Credit budget to launch.** Same DM + verification budget as #1 (different cohort) + Pitchlane / escript subscription (~$200/mo).

### 3. Newly-hired Credit Controller / Head of Finance / FD signal (score 22 × ease 4 = 88)

- **What it is.** Track UK construction firms in the 88K master that posted a finance / credit role in the last 60 days, via TheirStack. New hires in those roles are mandated to "review tools" in their first 30-60 days. Exact buying window. CreditSafe is the default they'll inherit; Grand needs to be the alternative on the review list before week 3.
- **Why it'll work.** Hot timing signal. Maps directly to Kirk's "T2 has an AI mandate" insight: newly-hired CFOs / FDs at T2 are exactly the cohort whose board has asked them to "get on top of AI in finance." Volume is the unknown: needs one free TheirStack probe to confirm UK construction × finance hiring count over 30 days.
- **Lead magnet.** M3 free 30-day risk audit of recipient's existing ledger + M4 £500 credits.
- **Build chain on sign-off.** `/lilly-theirstack-setup` with finance-hiring titles (Credit Controller, Credit Manager, Head of Credit, CFO, Head of Finance, Finance Director, Financial Controller, AR Manager) × UK + Cat 1 construction SIC codes. Icebreaker rule: `skip_angles=["Hiring", "You joined"]` per Navreo's hiring-signal-Icebreaker memory.
- **Probe before commit.** Fire one free TheirStack preview test query. If <20 fresh jobs/month, bundle as a 4th-touch sequence on Idea #1 instead of standalone.
- **Credit budget to launch.** ~60 Prospeo/month for DM enrichment of ~30 jobs × 2 DMs.
