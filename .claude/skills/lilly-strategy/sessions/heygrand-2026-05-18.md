## Recommended top 3 (where to start, week 1)

Ranked by **score × speed to launch** with Kirk's existing 88K list + Instantly stack:

### 1. Insolvency-alert list (score 25/25), **build first, launch this week**

**What it is:** Send a 4-line email to Credit Controllers and Heads of Finance at T2/T3 builders merchants, plant hire firms, and construction-product manufacturers. The email's payload is a free list of construction companies in their geographic area that went into administration or insolvency this week, pulled directly from Grand's platform via SIC-code + geocode + status-tag query.

**Why it'll work:**
- **Highest-fit signal of all 8 ideas.** Credit controllers' #1 KPI is "don't give credit to someone who's about to go bust." The lead magnet IS their job.
- **Reusable forever.** Insolvency data refreshes weekly, so the same campaign re-touches the list every week with fresh data. No "campaign exhaustion" problem.
- **Kirk confirmed deliverable RIGHT NOW** (transcript: "I can pull that list for them"). No engineering wait.
- **Bait-and-prove approach**, they get the list (delivers immediate value), and the email follows up with: "we identified these 48-72 hours before CreditSafe did. Want to see how?"
- Maps directly to Kirk's primary messaging anchor: stop-loss / risk-removal, not money savings.

**Lead magnet:** Insolvency list, geo-cut. Delivery = CSV per recipient region (Kirk's eng team has the query already).

**Build chain:**
1. Filter the 88K master to T2/T3 only, exclude the 4K MCR list.
2. Slice by sub-vertical (start with builders merchants, highest volume, clearest fit).
3. `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` filtered to Persona 1 titles (Credit Controller, Head of Credit, Head of Finance, Finance Director).
4. `/lilly-email-verification` (AI Ark primary, Prospeo for missing emails).
5. Push verified emails to Instantly (or Smartlead if call decides to migrate). Push no-email-found rows to HeyReach via `/lilly-heyreach-upload`.
6. Kirk's eng team produces a per-region insolvency CSV ahead of each send.

**Credit budget to launch:** ~200 Prospeo credits (DM enrichment of ~5K T2/T3 builders merchants) + ~50 AI Ark credits + 0 cost on the lead magnet (Grand's own data).

**Expected output:** If we hit ~5K builders merchants × Persona 1 (avg 2 contacts/co with verified email) = ~7-10K leads. At 50K/mo send and Navreo benchmarks (1% reply, 5-15% positive), that's 25-75 positives, comfortably exceeding the Q2 30-demo target.

### 2. Trade-credit-application bait + personalised video (score 22/25), **scale up the current winning play**

**What it is:** Kirk's currently winning email, "Hey, do you offer trade credit?", works because recipients assume Grand is a builder applying to them for trade credit, and the recipient's KPI is growing controlled trade-credit accounts. On positive reply, instead of pitching the platform (which kills the conversation when they realise we're not a buyer), trigger a per-recipient personalised video.

**Why it'll work:**
- **Proven mechanic.** Kirk has been running this manually with a few replies coming through. Just need to scale it from 2,000 leads to 40K/mo.
- **The bait-and-switch is the conversion lever.** Right now Kirk's losing replies the moment they realise it's not a trade-credit app. A personalised video that opens with "Hi {firstname}, I want to tell you how OrderD have been using Grand…" reframes the conversation cleanly, they expected an application, they got a peer testimonial.
- **Pitchlane and [escript](https://escript.com/) handle the name-swap at scale**, Kirk records one base video, the tool swaps the name and the on-screen LinkedIn-profile mention per recipient.

**Lead magnet:** The video itself + free credits + onboarding call (T2 = call-only, T3 = call-or-self-serve, both via Calendly).

**Build chain:**
1. Use the full 88K Cat 1 master minus 4K MCR list as the universe.
2. Same DM enrichment + email verification chain as Idea #1.
3. Send the trade-credit bait via Instantly.
4. On positive reply, trigger a Pitchlane / escript video send (manual step, until Kirk's team builds the auto-trigger).

**Credit budget to launch:** Same as Idea #1 (~200 Prospeo + ~50 AI Ark credits per sub-segment) + Pitchlane or escript subscription (~$200/mo).
