**Concept:** Lookalike-by-team-experience. Each campaign = ONE niche. We target companies that look like the marquee brands KRG's consultants have worked with in that niche, and the email positions the consultant's past client work as KRG's active, deployable experience. Each niche pulls the most relevant team case studies.

## How the market was sized
Every "reachable decision-makers" number below is a LIVE AI-Ark count (not an estimate) of senior communications / corporate-affairs leaders (C-suite / VP / Head / Director in the media & communication function) at 1,000+ employee companies in the niche, across the 10 core markets (US, UK, Canada, Germany, France, Netherlands, Switzerland, Ireland, Italy, Australia). This IS the KRG buyer. Niches far above 5,000 are capped to a best-fit ~5,000 slice (tighten to CCO/Head + top sub-sectors); niches near 5,000 run whole.

> Note on the "XYZ" in each email: the proof lines cite each consultant's REAL client experience, but any hard result stat (a %, a valuation) must be confirmed with that consultant before send. Nothing below invents a client outcome number.

## Recommended top 4 (where to start)
1. **Food & Beverage / CPG** — cleanest ~5,000 (no capping), deepest bench (6 consultants), marquee proof (Coca-Cola / Nestle / McDonald's / AB InBev). Pain is live: boycotts & culture-war flashpoints.
2. **Non-profit / NGO / Foundations** — whitespace almost no advisory courts, and the pain is acute right now (funding cuts, USAID-style shocks). Amnesty / UNDP / WHO / Red Cross / Greenpeace proof is unmatched. ~5,892.
3. **Financial Services (IR + activist-defence slice)** — sharp, high-value buyer with urgent, concrete pain; Richard Mahony's J.P. Morgan + activist-defence + Vivienne's fintech is a rare combination. Cap to ~5,000.
4. **Energy / Climate** — the seed niche behind the brief's own example; tight ~4,433; David Bloomgren's regulatory/project-approval angle + Chris Deri's shareholder/geopolitics angle.
