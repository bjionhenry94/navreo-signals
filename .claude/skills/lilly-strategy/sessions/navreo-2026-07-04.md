## Recommended top 3 (where to start)

### 1. Multi-thread the positive-replier companies (idea 2)
- **What it is:** 985 companies have already had someone reply positively to us. We already hold 2,353 OTHER contacts at those exact companies. Contact them with the proven pay-after-build offer, with an opener that references their team's interest area (never quoting the colleague's reply verbatim).
- **Why it works:** warmest possible cold audience: the company has demonstrated interest. Zero list-building cost, the data layer built this list for free.
- **Launch budget:** ~0 credits for the list (already ours), email verification only.

### 2. Claude Code 2nd touch on the 7 forgotten cohorts (idea 1)
- **What it is:** 2,511 tool-follower leads that got exactly one email in Jan/Mar and were never recontacted. Send them the Claude Code breakdown (June's best offer, which did not exist when they were first contacted).
- **Why it works:** proven mechanism (follower) x proven touch depth (2nd) x newest proven offer. Fatigue-safe: the 3rd-touch complaint spike does not apply to a 2nd touch.
- **Launch budget:** ~0 credits, leads already enriched.

### 3. Founding-sales / GTM-engineer hiring signal (idea 4)
- **What it is:** 489 companies per month hire their first dedicated sales/GTM person. Offer: we are the outbound system while (or instead of) that hire ramping. Loom + pay-after-build.
- **Why it works:** the hire IS the budget signal, and the offer directly substitutes what they are shopping for. TheirStack-verified monthly flow.
- **Launch budget:** TheirStack free previews + standard DM enrichment credits.

- Performance-marketing/PPC agencies with the GEO case study: adjacent to the April Performance Marketing campaign which produced 0 positives from 122 replies. Re-pitch only with a new named case study in that exact vertical.
- Inc 5000 / awards news intent: untested, deliverable, but weakest probe story; revisit when the August list drops.
- 3rd-touch recontacts of Clay/Instantly Jan cohorts: blocked by the 3rd-touch complaint-rate finding (60% DNC).
- 6sense/DemandBase ABM-followers deep recontact: 6sense already had 4 campaigns; diminishing.
