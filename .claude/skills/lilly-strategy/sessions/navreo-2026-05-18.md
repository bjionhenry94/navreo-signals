## Recommended top 3 (where to start)

Ranked by **(Total score × delivery ease for a 1-person ops team)**:

### 1. Recently-funded services firms — monthly news sweep  (V7.A, score 23)
- **What it is:** Pull a rolling list of services firms (agencies, consultancies, staffing, PR) that announced funding in the past 30 days via Serper news (Crunchbase + TechCrunch + PR newswire feeds), enrich DMs, run a per-lead opener tied to the funding amount + raise stage. The funding IS the buying signal: cash in the bank, 12-18 months runway to spend, GTM investment is the typical first deployment.
- **Why it'll work:** Highest-intent signal of all 10 ideas. Capacity proven (they raised), urgency proven (12-18 mo to deploy), category-fit proven (services firms raising = scaling client work). Serper test pulled 10 marketing-agency Series A stories in the last 30 days alone, so the rate across all services verticals + funding stages comfortably clears 100-200/mo.
- **Lead magnet:** **E. "Free LinkedIn-followers scrape of your top 3 competitors"** — shows capability, novel, low-friction delivery (use existing Lilly pipelines), low risk for a newly-funded firm to say yes.
- **Build chain:** `/lilly-icebreaker-news-search` (sets up the rolling news monitor), then `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` (one-time per cohort to enrich DMs), then `/lilly-bot` (Smartlead campaign with `{{Icebreaker}}` filled from the funding news).
- **Credit budget to launch:** ~50-100 Serper credits (initial 100-co batch) + ~50 Prospeo credits (DM enrichment) + monthly recurring spend ~30-50 credits.

### 2. Demand Gen / Growth hire signal  (V2.C, score 22)
- **What it is:** Track companies posting "Demand Generation", "Director of Growth", "Head of Growth", "VP Growth" roles in the past 30 days. These are the EXACT roles Navreo offsets as an alternative to. The job posting tells you exactly when the buying conversation starts.
- **Why it'll work:** TheirStack returned **797 jobs / 671 cos in 30 days** — daily flow of ~25 fresh prospects, sustainable monthly campaign at typical sender-volumes. Sample looks on-brief ("Head of Marketing, Consulting Industry"). Direct mapping from signal to offer: "you're hiring a Demand Gen lead — that role takes 6 months to ramp and $150K/year + tools. We deliver the same outcome in 3 weeks on performance."
- **Lead magnet:** **D. 100-lead sample of their ICP** — proves the system works on their actual market before they commit to a Demand Gen hire.
- **Build chain:** `/lilly-theirstack-setup` with the three angles tested in Phase 4 above.
- **Credit budget to launch:** 0 setup credits (TheirStack free previews already done) + ~150-200 Prospeo credits per month for DM enrichment of the ~25/day flow.

### 3. Engagers of cold-email thought-leaders  (V3.1, score 22)
- **What it is:** Track who engages with [Josh Braun](https://linkedin.com/in/josh-braun) (~200K followers), [Morgan J Ingram](https://linkedin.com/in/morganjingramamp) (194K), [Florin Tatulea](https://linkedin.com/in/florintatulea) (50K), [Jed Mahrle](https://linkedin.com/in/jedmahrle), [Will Allred](https://linkedin.com/in/will-allred). Engager = lead. Their audience IS B2B sales leaders thinking actively about outbound.
- **Why it'll work:** Direct audience-match — these thought leaders sell cold-email + sales training, so the engagement filter pre-qualifies people who care about outbound. With ~300 engagers/profile/mo, 5 profiles = ~1500 fresh engagers/mo. Once de-duped and ICP-filtered, expect ~400-600/mo qualified leads.
- **Lead magnet:** **E. Competitor followers scrape** — credibility play, shows you can do what they probably already follow Josh Braun about.
- **Build chain:** `/lilly-trigify-setup` with the 5 profiles + `skip_angles=["Hiring","You joined"]` to avoid stalker-coded openers per the icebreaker rule.
- **Credit budget to launch:** ~20 Trigify credits + ~150 Prospeo credits/mo enrichment.

## Notes for the conversation with yourself / CSM

1. **V1.B "broad B2B services" was a planning failure** — the "agency" + "consultancy" keyword combo pulled mostly staffing firms because LinkedIn's company-keyword index for those terms skews to staffing. Lesson: even for broad TAM probes, use `company_industry` enum strings (which Prospeo validates strictly) instead of soft keywords.

2. **V2.B "VP/Head Sales hire 50-200 emp" had off-brief titles in the sample** (Head of Renewable Sales — that's an energy company). Before going live, the TheirStack saved-search needs `job_title_not` exclusions for industry-specific Sales roles (Renewable, Insurance, Real Estate, Pharma).

3. **The 3 existing TheirStack briefs (GTM / SDR / VP-Head Sales)** were ignored per the greenfield directive — but in production you'd cross-check angles 2, 3, 7 against those briefs and skip any duplicates. The user explicitly opted out of this filter this run.

4. **All 4 named clients fit the broader pattern** but the lookalike clusters per seed (V6) will be small (150-600 cos each, ~1500 total). For headline-TAM campaigns, V1.C marketing/ad agencies (37K cos) is the size leader. For high-intent campaigns, V7.A funded-services and V3.x engagement signals dominate.

5. **Total estimated credit budget if you ran all top 3 in parallel for month 1:** ~400-500 credits across Prospeo / AI Ark / Serper / Trigify. Within the typical Navreo client onboarding spend envelope.
