## Top 3 — where to start

Ranked by **(Total score × delivery friction × novelty for Navreo)**. All three are NET-NEW campaign types Prospeo V2.1 makes possible — they cannot be replicated cleanly via Ocean.io, AI Ark, or TheirStack alone.

### 1. Newly-appointed VP Sales / CRO at services firms (Key Executive Events) — [NET-NEW]

- **What it is:** Prospeo's Key Executive Events filter surfaces companies where a VP Sales / CRO / Head of Sales was appointed in the last 30-180 days. Layer on Type (Agency/Consulting) + Subtype (Software Development / AI Services / Digital Transformation) + Headcount 51-200 to isolate Navreo's ICP at its hottest buying moment.
- **Why it'll work:** A new sales leader has a 90-day window to prove pipeline impact. Tools and systems get audited and replaced. Navreo's DFY offer maps 1:1 to "first-90-day pipeline build" — the EXACT mandate they're under.
- **Lead magnet:** "Newly-appointed sales leader's first-90-day pipeline playbook" PDF + offer of free 30-min strategy session
- **Example named companies that fit the filter shape:** [Thoughtworks](https://linkedin.com/company/thoughtworks), [Slalom](https://linkedin.com/company/slalom), [Faculty AI](https://linkedin.com/company/faculty-ai), [West Monroe](https://linkedin.com/company/west-monroe-partners) (verify against Prospeo probe — these are illustrative)
- **Build chain:** `lilly-tam-mapper` (Prospeo `/search-company` with Key Executive Events + Type filter) → `lilly-decision-maker-finder` (target the new VP Sales directly, not the founder) → `lilly-bot`
- **Credit budget to launch:** ~10-20 Prospeo cr (5-10 pages of cos × 1 cr/page) + DM enrichment (~1 cr per company)
- **Probe-to-validate (1 cr):** Query `Type=Agency/Consulting + Key Executive Events=[VP Sales appointed last 60d] + Headcount 51-200 + Geo=high-GDP 14`

### 2. Agencies mentioning Apollo / Smartlead / Outreach / Lemlist / Clay on their website (Competitive Intel) — [NET-NEW]

- **What it is:** Prospeo's Competitive Intel filter surfaces companies that mention a specific competitor's brand on their website. Pull Navreo's category-aware buyer cohort by searching for cos that mention any major outbound tool (Apollo, Smartlead, Outreach, Lemlist, Clay, Saleshandy, Reply, Lavender) on their site.
- **Why it'll work:** These cos are already running outbound. Education cost is zero. Two clean messaging angles: (a) "your stack's broken, here's the fix" (agencies struggling with DIY) or (b) "you've outgrown DIY, outsource the heavy lifting" (mid-stage agencies hitting capacity).
- **Lead magnet:** "Free outbound stack audit — we'll review your [tool] setup and flag what's leaking pipeline"
- **Build chain:** `lilly-tam-mapper` (Prospeo Competitive Intel filter) → `lilly-decision-maker-finder` (VP Sales / Head of GTM / Founder) → `lilly-bot`
- **Credit budget to launch:** ~8-15 Prospeo cr + DM enrichment
- **Probe-to-validate (1 cr):** Query `Type=Agency + Competitive Intel mentions=[Apollo, Smartlead, Outreach, Lemlist, Clay] + Headcount 51-200 + Geo=high-GDP 14`
- **Note:** Skip mentioning Navreo's own competitor names (Belkins / Cognism / ColdIQ) in messaging — agencies mentioning THOSE are running the alt-outsource motion already and Navreo's offer is direct competitive

### 3. Agencies counting OpenAI / Anthropic / Salesforce / Microsoft / AWS / Google as customers (Key Customers) — [NET-NEW]

- **What it is:** Prospeo's Key Customers filter (UNIQUE — no competitor has this) returns companies that publicly claim a named brand as a customer. Search for big-name customers like OpenAI, Anthropic, Salesforce, Microsoft, AWS, Google, Adobe, Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot — surfaces enterprise-grade agencies who serve Fortune 500-tier clients.
- **Why it'll work:** Agencies that win enterprise clients have (a) the price point Navreo needs for unit economics, (b) the sales-cycle complexity that drives outbound investment, and (c) concentrated-revenue churn fear (one big-name leaving = catastrophe). The lead magnet is dead-simple to pitch: "You serve [Enterprise X]. You can't depend on them forever. Here's how to systematically win 3 more like them per quarter."
- **Lead magnet:** Case study + free pipeline audit. CTA: "Want our [comparable agency] case study + a free 30-min audit of your current pipeline?"
- **Example big-name customers to query:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Salesforce, Microsoft, AWS, Google, Adobe, Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot, Atlassian, Nvidia, Meta, ServiceNow, Snowflake
- **Build chain:** `lilly-tam-mapper` (Prospeo Key Customers filter) → `lilly-decision-maker-finder` → `lilly-bot`
- **Credit budget to launch:** ~10-20 Prospeo cr + DM enrichment
- **Probe-to-validate (1 cr):** Query `Type=Agency/Consulting + Key Customers=[OpenAI, Anthropic, Salesforce, Microsoft, AWS, Google] + Headcount 51-200`

## Cross-cutting upgrades (apply to ALL existing Navreo campaigns)

Three V2.1 features that aren't campaigns themselves but compound across every campaign Navreo is already running.

| Upgrade | What it does | When to apply |
|---|---|---|
| **Smart Job Title (Loose / Normal / Strict)** | AI-expands "VP Sales" into all real-world variants ("Head of GTM", "Director of Revenue", "VP of Sales Operations", "Growth Lead"). 20-40% lift on DM coverage vs. hand-built title arrays | Retrofit every active `lilly-decision-maker-finder` call. Already-built campaigns: re-run DM enrichment with Smart Job Title=Loose on the existing company lists, expect +20-40% net-new DMs |
| **Company Lookalike — description mode** | No seed domain needed. Describe the niche in plain English; Prospeo's vector search returns matching cos. Unlocks edge-case verticals where seed cos are unknown or thin | Use in Phase 2 of `lilly-tam-mapper` when seed-domain clusters are weak. Also great for ad-hoc strategy-call ideation ("describe me a 'low-code AI consultancy that sells to mid-market manufacturing'") |
| **AI Search (plain English → filters)** | Translates a brief into filters automatically. Faster ideation; no manual filter construction. Available as MCP for Claude — fits directly into `lilly-tam-mapper` Stage 2 | Use in Phase 0 of any new TAM build to speed first-pass filter construction. Validate the filters before paginating (don't trust the AI translation blindly) |
