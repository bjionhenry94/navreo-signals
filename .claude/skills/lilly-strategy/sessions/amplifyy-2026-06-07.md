## Recommended top 3 (where to start)
Ranked by score × low delivery-friction.

### 1. Brands actively hiring for Amazon / Seller Central
- **What it is:** Catch brands the moment they post a job mentioning Amazon Seller Central / marketplace / a Head-of-Amazon role. A brand staffing the channel is explicitly committing budget to it right now — the single hottest intent signal available for this audience.
- **Why it'll work:** Probe = 261 fresh companies/month (Seller Central in job description) + 256/mo (Amazon/marketplace title). The job post itself is the icebreaker hook ("saw you're building out your Amazon team"). Pitch = we run it for you on a performance basis so you don't have to hire.
- **Lead magnet:** Free done-for-you Amazon account teardown (we audit their listings/ads and show what's leaking, we do the work — never "send us your process"). Gift-card stays out of this email.
- **Build chain:** `/lilly-theirstack-setup` (the TheirStack → Make → Smartlead pipeline already exists) → `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` per new company → `/lilly-icebreaker` with `skip_angles=["Hiring","You joined"]` (mandatory — the signal IS hiring, so don't also open on it).
- **Credit budget to launch:** ~0 to set up the saved search (TheirStack previews free); enrichment ~1 Prospeo credit per new company per day thereafter.

### 2. Brands that just appointed a new C-level leader
- **What it is:** Layer `company_key_execs` (Any C-Level Appointed, last 180 days) onto the EXISTING locked e-commerce base. New leadership = reorg + fresh budget + a mandate to prove wins fast.
- **Why it'll work:** 1,150 companies sit on this signal right now, all already inside your validated e-comm-brand base — zero new infrastructure, just one extra filter on the Prospeo pull you already run. Open on the leadership change ("congrats on the new CMO").
- **Lead magnet:** Category Amazon benchmark report (their category's average conversion / ad efficiency vs theirs) — a value-first play a new leader can take to their team.
- **Build chain:** existing Prospeo base + `company_key_execs:{event_types:["Any C-Level Appointed"],timeframe_days:180}` → `/lilly-decision-maker-finder` → `/lilly-icebreaker` (`skip_angles=["You joined"]`).
- **Credit budget:** ~1 Prospeo credit/page on the existing pull. Negligible incremental.

### 3. Engagers of Amazon-seller thought leaders
- **What it is:** Track posts from named Amazon-seller voices; the people who like/comment are operators actively thinking about their Amazon channel. The engager is the lead — no company list, no DM-finder.
- **Why it'll work:** Warm, self-identified audience. Named profiles to track: [Steven Pope (My Amazon Guy)](https://linkedin.com/in/stevenpopemag), [Bradley Sutton (Helium 10)](https://linkedin.com/in/bradleysutton), [Destaney Wishon (BetterAMS)](https://linkedin.com/in/destaneywishon), [Liran Hirschkorn (Incrementum)](https://linkedin.com/in/liranhirschkorn), Kevin King. Estimate ~150–250 engagers/post, several posts/week each.
- **Fit caveat:** engager pools mix in other agencies / tiny arbitrage sellers — needs a brand-vs-non-brand gate at processing (same hard gate as the lists: is-a-real-product-brand).
- **Build chain:** `/lilly-trigify-setup` (tracked-profile slugs above) → `/lilly-icebreaker` with `skip_angles=["Hiring","You joined"]`.
- **Credit budget:** Trigify subscription cost (outside Lilly credits) + ~0 Prospeo (engager email via enrich-person, ~1cr each).

## Brainstormed but cut

- **News / funding intent (Vector 7) — CUT.** Probed live: recently-funded e-comm brands = **59** (365d), recent product-launch/expansion news = **86**, funding/award news = **118**. Prospeo's news/funding index covers VC-tracked SaaS, not small consumer brands, so the pool is too thin to size a campaign. Re-pitch only if we source funding via a D2C-specific feed (e.g. a Shopify-Plus or CPG-funding newsletter), not Prospeo.
- **Marketing-specific exec change (CMO/VP-Marketing Appointed) — folded into #2.** At 90d it returned NO_RESULTS; "Any C-Level Appointed" at 180d is the buildable version, so we use the broad event and gate by title at enrichment.

We don't have those named yet — **ask Kevin for his 5 best brands** before building, otherwise it collapses back into the targeted-list mechanism already live.
