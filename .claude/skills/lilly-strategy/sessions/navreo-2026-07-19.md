## Recommended top 3 (where to start)

### 1. Apollo.io followers
- **What it is:** People who follow Apollo.io on LinkedIn — 151,300 of them — already run or care about outbound. The follower is the lead; no decision-maker hunt needed.
- **Why it'll work:** every follower audience we've run lands 3.8-9.4 positives per 1,000 (Clay, Instantly, AmpleMarket, Smartlead). Apollo is the biggest source we've never touched.
- **Lead magnet:** two-minute teardown video of the outbound engine we'd bolt onto the stack they already run; free-pilot P.S.
- **Build chain on sign-off:** follower export → /lilly-tam netting → /lilly-idea-to-launch (adapt proven Clay-followers copy, same author, swap tool vocabulary).
- **Approx. credit budget to launch:** scrape cost external (Trigify/PB); provider credits only for email fill on fresh rows, wave-based 300s.

### 2. Sales-hire hiring signal
- **What it is:** Companies posting sales jobs right now (8,747 fresh postings/30d in our geos). We email the founder/sales leader while the new hire ramps.
- **Why it'll work:** hiring = live budget + live pipeline pain; we've never run this at scale (231 sends total across both prior signal campaigns) so the lane is clean.
- **Lead magnet:** "the outbound system your new hire inherits" one-pager; pay-per-lead framing.
- **Build chain on sign-off:** /lilly-theirstack-setup brief (describe-then-translate title patterns) → daily /lilly-theirstack-data-processing; skip_angles=["Hiring","You joined"] passes to lilly-icebreaker.
- **Approx. credit budget to launch:** TheirStack previews free; Prospeo enrich on fresh DMs only, daily drip.

### 3. Collections / AR-recovery firms
- **What it is:** 426 reachable owners/sales leaders at collections and receivables-recovery firms. Never contacted as a segment.
- **Why it'll work:** top scorer of the whole niche lab (24/30); their prospects live the pain daily (overdue invoices never go quiet), and the offer is a no-brainer ("meetings with finance chiefs sitting on aged receivables").
- **Lead magnet:** mapped plan + named-targets sample ("2-3 names we'd go after first, free").
- **Build chain on sign-off:** /lilly-tam wave pull (free-from-records first) → ListMint ≤50 batches → /lilly-upload-gate → Smartlead.
- **Approx. credit budget to launch:** ~290 paid-fresh rows × enrich hit-rate; ~136 rows free from our records.
