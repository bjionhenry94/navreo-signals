**The two gates (from brief):**

## Probed and cut

- **Employer-of-record / global payroll / PEOs** (probe 1,509): probe pool was almost entirely staffing firms (already-tried vertical); the genuine pure-plays (Deel, Remote, Velocity Global) are 500+ staff with in-house SDR teams. Cut.
- **Corporate training / L&D firms** (probe 950): passes the audience gate (every mid-size+ company buys training) but fails ticket consistency: a $2k workshop seller and a $100k leadership-program firm look identical from outside. Only worth running as a tight sub-cut (enterprise leadership-program firms), not as a niche.

## The reusable 4-question filter (how these were picked)

1. **Horizontal buyer universe?** Do they sell to "every business that has X" (staff, shipments, energy bills, insurance needs) rather than a narrow vertical?
2. **Ticket structurally visible?** Recurring contract, project minimum, regulated commission, or success fee baked into the business model, so no outside-in guessing needed.
3. **Do they already buy meetings?** MSPs, insurance producers, M&A originators, and freight brokers all already pay for appointment-setting, so pay-on-performance is a familiar purchase, not a new concept.
4. **Is their outbound offer a no-brainer?** (Added by Bjion same day.) The cold ask the niche can make must be near-universally relevant and frictionless for the recipient: "Would you consider selling your business?" (M&A), "We'll buy your old equipment" (ITAD), "Send one lane, we'll quote it cheaper" (freight). If the niche's natural ask requires the prospect to be mid-project or to commit before seeing value, meetings are harder to book and the pay-per-meeting economics suffer.

## Addendum 3 (2026-06-11): final gate set + master scoreboard

**Gate set (final form, per Bjion):**
- **G1 Size:** 10,000+ findable decision-makers at ~1.5 per company (= ~6,700+ companies in-index).
- **G2 Ticket:** deal size structurally high AND visible from outside (recurring contracts, project minimums, commissions, success fees).
- **G3 Offer:** their natural cold ask is a no-brainer (near-universally relevant, frictionless).
- **G4 They already do cold (bonus):** industries that already run or buy cold outreach close faster on a pay-per-meeting pitch.
- **G5 Recipient receptivity (KILL gate):** the niche's own prospect audience must actually respond to cold email. Cybersecurity is KILLED here per Bjion: security buyers never convert on cold email (most-pitched persona in B2B, channel burned). Commercial lending also effectively killed: "you're approved for $X" financing is the most spam-burned cold category (MCA spam legacy).

## Addendum 6 (2026-06-11): Gate 6 (pain frequency) + wave 6

**Bjion's new dynamic, codified as G6 Pain frequency:** how often is the recipient ACTIVELY living the problem the niche solves? Episodic/dormant needs (pest infestations, inspections due, roof replacement) force huge volume with low instantaneous hit rates. Always-on pains (overdue invoices, denied claims, missed calls, daily ticket backlogs) mean the recipient is in the problem the day the email lands. Scoring is now /30; the floor scales from 15/25 to **18/30** (same 60%). Commercial roofing APPROVED and tasked before this gate landed (G6 would be ~3, mitigated by the free-inspection ask and storm triggers).
