## Strategic read (volume vs intent)
Tightening to 100-1000 headcount shrank the high-intent pools hard: A6 = 211 cos, A9 = 250, A8 = 507, B4 = 568, A7 = 1,493. Against Arnic's ~75k/month capacity these are **evergreen / refreshing** campaigns (e.g. ~200 new VP-Sales appointments land per 90 days), not volume fillers. Volume has to come from the **sales-led list (32k cos)** — but that needs a `company_industry` exclude to strip the staffing/media leak before it's worth running. Recommended portfolio: run the intent signals always-on + a tightened sales-led list for fill.
