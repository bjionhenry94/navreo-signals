## Base (constant across all ideas)
- **Definition:** `company_type.is_retail = true` **AND** a 13-industry consumer-product whitelist. Highest-precision "makes-its-own-products consumer brand" base.
- **Base refined mid-session:** started on the 13-industry whitelist alone (85,154), but there is no literal "Ecommerce" industry in Prospeo (`E-Commerce`/`Ecommerce` are INVALID). Prospeo's native `is_retail` flag (110,798) is the closest equivalent. Intersecting `is_retail` AND the whitelist = 23,478 brands and a 16/16-clean sample (it strips the B2B "networks" the whitelist-only base leaked). User chose this precision base over the broader retail-flag-only option.
- **Why not the Amazon keyword/integration filters:** they surface the agency/tool/3PL ecosystem (Pattern, Helium 10, Jungle Scout, ShipBob), not the brands. Consumer brands of this size almost universally sell on Amazon; Amazon presence is verified at enrichment, not pre-filtered.
- **Industry whitelist (13):** Cosmetics, Personal Care Product Mfg, Food & Beverage Mfg, Food & Beverage Services, Retail Apparel & Fashion, Apparel Mfg, Consumer Goods, Sporting Goods Mfg, Wellness & Fitness, Online & Mail-Order Retail, Retail Groceries, Retail Health & Personal Care, Furniture & Home Furnishings Mfg.
- **Size:** 11-500 employees. **Geo:** US + UK + Canada + Australia (high-GDP English-first).
- **DM titles (N=4):** Head of E-commerce, Head of Marketplaces/Amazon, Head of Digital/Growth, CMO/Head of Marketing.
- **DM TAM = Brands x 4.** TARGET reach (≈1 contact per title per brand), NOT guaranteed-findable. Actual findable count confirmed at the `lilly-decision-maker-finder` enrichment step (Prospeo runs ~0.3-0.7 findable/brand for SMB/mid-market).

## Probe-confirmed findings
- **Only three ideas have standalone-campaign volume:** #6 surging traffic (8,543 brands), #7 high traffic (1,765), #3 leadership change (311). Everything else (16-66 brands) is a micro-segment to layer in or drip, not a 50k-scale campaign.
- **Web traffic is Prospeo's strongest signal for this ICP** — surge (#6) and absolute volume (#7) are the only signals with real depth.
- **Funding-round signal is dead** (1 brand). Use the **venture-backed attribute (#8, 107)** as the "funded" proxy.
- **DTC tool-stack signal is dead** (6 brands). Prospeo doesn't index Klaviyo/Gorgias/Recharge for consumer brands. Needs a Shopify-app data source.
- **Precision/volume trade-off:** this base (23,478) is the cleanest (16/16 brand sample) but smallest. The broader `is_retail`-only base (110,798) would roughly double every number if volume is the constraint.
