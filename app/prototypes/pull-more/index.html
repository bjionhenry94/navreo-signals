Pull-more UX lab

Five ways to add more people to a campaign from its pool. All pulls here are simulated — nothing touches live data or spends credits. Baseline: the shipped Sources-tab panel (7.2 / 7.0 / 8.4).
P1 · Preset chipsTap 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 — no typing, two taps total. P2 · Top up to targetPick where the campaign should end up; the tool does the math. Progress bar to target. P3 · Keep it topped upA standing weekly rule with one switch — pulling stops being a task. "Add now" button for today. P4 · List-row quick actionAdd people straight from the campaigns list; the row shows progress. No page visit. P5 · One sentence"Add [500] more verified people." One button.
