- **No prototype cleared the 9/10-from-all-5 bar.** Reporting true scores; nothing inflated.
- **Winner: P3 · Gap-to-target scorecard** — 8.4/8.2/8.2 avg, unanimous favourite R2 + R3,

- Why P3 stalled at 8s not 9s: judges wanted the `→` to name WHICH follow-up email
  (addressed post-panel by matching P1's "rewrite the follow-up that asks for the call") and
  a hair of "which number first" at the very first glance (the WELL UNDER red chip resolves it).
